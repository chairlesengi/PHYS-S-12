Week 7: MVP

For my MVP, I built a closed-loop position feedback actuator using a stepper driver and a AS5600 12 bit magnetic angle sensor. It reads the sensor, determines the error between where it is and where it wants to be, and generates a proportional response signal. The actuator also has a 12:1 cycloidal drive reducer gearbox that multiplies the torque of the motor, while reducing its speed. The sensor connects over I2C, communicating the absolute angle of the stepper motor within one degree. Then, a custom AS5600Sensor class measures and keeps track of the position of the stepper over multiple rotations, up to 12000 rpm or so. A orchestrator 'Actuator' class then handles the feedback loop, commanding control into a modular motor class. The full libary can be found here.
